Shows which of your items, prisoners, slaves and animals a settlement will buy, and lets you form a caravan with them already selected.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]"Things you can sell" button on settlements, and in the vanilla "Things this trader will buy" window
[*]Lists only what you have that the trader wants
[*]Shows how many you have, market value and estimated sell price
[*]Pick a negotiator to see prices with their skill; they're added to the caravan
[*]Only colonists allowed to trade there can negotiate, e.g. the Empire's required title
[*]Tabs for items, prisoners and slaves, and animals
[*]Search, and sort by value or name
[*]Choose amounts, press "Form caravan", and the caravan window opens with them selected and the settlement set as the destination
[/list]

You can't trade from this window, it is a planning tool.
You will have to add colonists and travel supplies in the caravan window as usual.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from existing saves
[*]"Form caravan" is disabled for hostile settlements
[/list]
