[h1]Know what to pack before you leave[/h1]
The vanilla "Show what will buy" lists everything a settlement trades in, whether you own it or not. This mod checks that list against your stockpiles, prisoners, slaves and animals, and shows only what you actually have that they'll buy: how many, what it's worth, and what they'll pay.

Pick what to sell, press "Form caravan", and it's already selected.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/metarima/WhatCanISell/main/Workshop/1-button.jpg[/img]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]A "Things you can sell" button on settlements, and in the vanilla "Things this trader will buy" window
[*]Lists only what you have that the trader wants, with market value and estimated sell price
[*]Pick a negotiator to see prices with their skill; they're added to the caravan
[*]Tabs for items, prisoners and slaves, and animals, plus search and sorting
[*]A "won't buy" list showing the nearest settlement that will take the rest
[*]Press "Form caravan" and the caravan window opens with everything selected and the route set
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/metarima/WhatCanISell/main/Workshop/2-sell-list.jpg[/img]

[h1]Find a buyer for the rest[/h1]
Switch to "won't buy" to see what this settlement turns down, and the nearest settlement that buys it, with distance and travel time. Set how far to look, and click a buyer to find it on the map.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/metarima/WhatCanISell/main/Workshop/3-wont-buy.jpg[/img]

[h1]Straight into a caravan[/h1]
Your picks, the exact animals you chose and your negotiator are selected, and the destination is already set.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/metarima/WhatCanISell/main/Workshop/4-caravan.jpg[/img]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
- Can I trade from this window? - No, it is a planning tool. You will have to add colonists and travel supplies in the caravan window as usual.
- Are the prices exact? - Same formula as the trade screen, using the negotiator you pick. The final price depends on who actually negotiates.
- Why is "Form caravan" greyed out? - The faction is hostile, or nobody in your colony can trade there (the Empire needs a title).
- Can I add or remove it mid-save? - Yes. It adds nothing to your save.
- Does it cost performance? - No. It does nothing until you open its window.
